解线性方程组：
$$
\begin{cases} 
(1 + a_1)x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n = b_1, \\
x_1 + (1 + a_2)x_2 + \cdots + x_n = b_2, \\
\vdots \\
x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + (1 + a_n)x_n = b_n,
\end{cases}
$$
其中 $a_i \neq 0$，且 $\sum_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{a_i} \neq -1$。

增广矩阵为：
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
1+a_1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 & b_1\\
1 & 1+a_2 & \cdots & 1 & b_2\\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots\\
1 & 1 & \cdots & 1+a_n & b_n
\end{array}\right)
$$
化简：
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
1+a_1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 & b_1\\
-a_1 & a_2 & \cdots & 0 & b_2-b_1\\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots\\
-a_1 & 0 & \cdots & a_n & b_n-b_1
\end{array}\right)
$$
再化简
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
1+a_1+\frac{a_1}{a_2}+\cdots+\frac{a_1}{a_n} & 0 & \cdots & 0 & b_1+\frac{b_1-b_2}{a_2}+\cdots+\frac{b_1-b_n}{a_n}\\
-a_1 & a_2 & \cdots & 0 & b_2-b_1\\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots\\
-a_1 & 0 & \cdots & a_n & b_n-b_1
\end{array}\right)
$$

即
$$
\left(\begin{array}{cccc|c}
a_1(1+\frac{1}{a_1}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_n}) & 0 & \cdots & 0 & b_1(1+\frac{1}{a_1}+\cdots+\frac{1}{a_n})-(\frac{b_1}{a_1}+\cdots+\frac{b_1}{a_1})\\
-a_1 & a_2 & \cdots & 0 & b_2-b_1\\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots\\
-a_1 & 0 & \cdots & a_n & b_n-b_1
\end{array}\right)
$$

 记 $S = \sum \frac{1}{a_i}$，$T = \sum \frac{b_i}{a_i}$ ，则
$$
\boxed{
x_i = \frac{b_i}{a_i} - \frac{1}{a_i}\cdot\frac{T}{1+S}, \quad i=1,2,\cdots,n.
}
$$
